How Primordia works

Each organism has a genome of four traits. Every tick it spends energy just to stay alive. Speed, vision and size all add to that cost. Eating restores energy. With enough stored energy, an adult splits in two, and its child inherits its genes, sometimes with small random mutations.

No code chooses the winners. Organisms whose traits suit the current world gather more energy than they burn, so they have more children and their genes spread. That is natural selection.

    Ecology: food grows mostly in drifting fertile zones, and their dense centres are tall grass where predators struggle to spot prey. Seasons change how fast food grows. Herbivores that starve leave carrion, which predators scavenge.
